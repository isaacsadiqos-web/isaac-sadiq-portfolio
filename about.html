Biography

I am Isaac Sadiq, a B.Sc. Cybersecurity student at MIVA Open University. My journey into technology began with electrical engineering, where I earned a National Diploma and learned to approach problems the way an engineer does: break the system down, test each part and fix what is failing.

As more of daily life moved online, I became interested in a bigger question: how do we keep digital systems and the people who rely on them safe? That curiosity led me to cybersecurity. I am building my foundations in threats, safe practices and computer systems, with the goal of becoming a cybersecurity analyst.

I also enjoy the creative and business side of technology. I edit video, design graphics, create content and manage social media, and I provide IT support and technical services. These skills help me explain technical topics in plain language and understand how organisations use digital tools to grow.

Whatever I am working on, I try to follow the principle that guides me: be good, be better and be best.

Career aspirations

Where I am heading.

Two complementary paths: protecting digital systems and helping businesses use technology wisely.

Cybersecurity Analyst

Analysts watch over an organisation's systems, spot suspicious activity and help respond before it becomes a problem.

Typical responsibilities

  • Monitoring systems and alerts for unusual activity
  • Investigating and documenting security incidents
  • Helping assess vulnerabilities and recommend fixes
  • Promoting security awareness among staff and users

What draws me in

The detective-like problem solving, and the chance to protect people who may never know the threat existed.

Digital Business Consultant

Consultants help organisations choose and use digital tools, improve their online presence and grow with confidence.

Typical responsibilities

  • Understanding a client's goals and digital needs
  • Advising on online presence, content and brand identity
  • Recommending practical, secure technology solutions
  • Helping teams adopt new tools and processes

What draws me in

Connecting creative skills with technology and security to help small businesses succeed online.

My learning roadmap

These are planned steps toward my goals, not achievements to date.

  1. Build strong cybersecurity fundamentals through my degree.
  2. Deepen my knowledge of computer systems and networks.
  3. Practise with hands-on exercises and small security awareness projects.
  4. Work toward recognised industry certifications when the time is right.
  5. Apply my technical, creative and business skills in real roles.
